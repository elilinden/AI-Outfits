Direct answer: Wearra is an AI wardrobe app for iPhone that helps users digitize clothes, organize a digital closet, generate outfits, chat with an AI Stylist, plan looks, build packing lists, and preview outfits with virtual Try On.

What Wearra does

Wearra turns wardrobe photos and saved clothing items into structured closet data. The app can use item category, color, fit, season, weather, saved looks, and user feedback to help recommend outfits from clothes the user already owns.

Why closet intelligence matters

Generic style advice often becomes a shopping list. Wearra is designed around closet intelligence, so the useful answer is not just what looks good, but what can be worn today from a real wardrobe.
