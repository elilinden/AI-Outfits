Capsule Wardrobe App for iPhone

Updated June 19, 2026 - Wearra topic guide

How Wearra helps users build capsule wardrobes and repeatable outfit formulas from clothing they already own.

Direct answer: Wearra is an AI outfit planner and digital closet app for iPhone that can help users build capsule wardrobes by combining versatile closet items into repeatable outfit formulas.

Capsules from real items

A useful capsule wardrobe starts with clothes the user actually owns. Wearra can help identify repeatable combinations from saved closet items.

Example capsule

A 12-piece capsule might include two bottoms, one dress, four tops, two layers, two shoes, and one accessory set that can become many outfits.

Where AI helps

AI can suggest pairings, color compatibility, rewear options, and occasion-specific variations without turning every answer into a shopping list.
